Behind the Frame

Shot during service, not staged after it.

Every image here was taken in a working room with guests in it. Nothing is styled for the camera and no dish is rebuilt afterwards.

We photograph one service a season across the seven branches. The plates you see are the plates that went out, lit by the room's own fixtures, which is why some of them are darker than a brochure would allow.

On Film

One service, once a season.

We photograph a single evening across the seven rooms each season, then live with those images until the next one.

It keeps the archive honest. A room shot in February looks like February, with the menu that was actually running and the guests who actually booked. Nothing is re-shot because a dish changed or a table looked better empty.

The photographer works the whole service without a light kit. If an image is dark, that is what the room looked like at nine o'clock.
