Before You Book

Four things worth knowing.

01

Deposits

Only the chef table and private rooms take a deposit, and it comes straight off your final bill.

02

Cancellations

Free to change or cancel up to 48 hours ahead. Inside that window the deposit is retained.

03

Dietary notes

Give us 48 hours and the kitchen rebuilds the menu around you rather than removing courses.

04

Large parties

Nine guests or more moves to the private dining team, who will plan the menu with you directly.

Need Something Else?

Bigger table, private room, or a whole floor.

Parties over eight are handled by the events desk with a dedicated lead who stays in the room for the evening.

After You Book

What happens between now and the table.

Immediately

Written confirmation

A reference number, the room, and the exact time. Keep it, because every later change is handled against that reference.

Morning of

Kitchen briefing

Your dietary notes and occasion reach the full brigade and floor team at the eleven o'clock briefing, before anyone starts cooking.

On arrival

The table is already set

Seating preference applied, allergies confirmed quietly at the table rather than announced, and any occasion timed to land when you asked.

Next morning

One short survey

Four questions, no incentive to answer. It goes straight to the kitchen and the floor, unfiltered and with your name attached if you leave one.
