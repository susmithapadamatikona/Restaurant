Common Requests

Things you can sort out in one message.

01

Change a booking

Send the reference from your confirmation with the new date or guest count. No need to cancel and rebook, and nothing is charged inside the 48 hour window if you simply move it.

02

Flag an allergy

Reply to your confirmation email with the detail. It attaches to the booking, reaches the kitchen at the morning briefing, and stays on your profile for next time.

03

Mark an occasion

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals. Tell us the name and the moment you want it to land, and the floor team will handle the rest quietly.

Press & Partnerships

Working with us on something.

We take a small number of collaborations a year and turn down most of what is offered, usually for reasons of time rather than fit.

Press enquiries: press@stackly.example. Kit includes high-resolution imagery, chef portraits, room plans, and current menus.

Guest chef collaborations: planned six to nine months out. Send a menu outline rather than a deck.

Suppliers: we visit before we buy. Send what you grow, where, and how much of it, and expect a slow but honest answer.

Filming: possible outside service hours only, and never in a room with guests in it.
