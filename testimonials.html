How We Collect This

No incentives, no filtering.

01

One email, the morning after

Every direct booking gets a four-question survey. No discount for completing it, because a paid review is not a review.

02

Read as a team, weekly

Results go to the kitchen and the floor every Monday, unedited and with names attached where the guest left one.

03

Nothing gets taken down

We publish the three and four star responses alongside the five star ones. The only edits are for length and personal detail.

What Changed Because of It

Feedback that actually moved something.

Acoustics

The bar moved

Enough guests mentioned hearing the ice well from table nine that we relocated the entire bar during the 2023 rebuild.

Pacing

Course four slowed down

Repeated notes about the middle of the tasting menu feeling rushed. We added eight minutes and stopped losing the room there.

Access

Menus in large print

One guest asked once. Every branch now carries large-print and braille menus without anyone having to request them.
