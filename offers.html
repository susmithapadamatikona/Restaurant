The Small Print

Terms we would rather state up front.

Offers exist to fill quieter services, not to disguise the real price of dinner. Here is exactly how they work.

One offer per booking, and offers do not stack with membership discounts.

Offer tables are released twenty-eight days ahead and are capped per service.

Deposits still apply to the chef table and private dining, and come off the final bill.

Offers are withdrawn on public holidays and during residency evenings.

Claiming One

Three steps, no codes to remember.

01

Name it when you book

Write the offer into the notes field on the reservation form, or say it on the phone. There is no voucher code to enter.

02

We confirm before you arrive

The concierge desk checks availability against the cap for that service and confirms in writing the same day.

03

It comes off the bill

Applied automatically at the end of the evening. Nothing to present at the table and nothing to claim back afterwards.
