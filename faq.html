01

Before You Arrive

What is the dress code?

Smart casual. Jackets are welcome but never required, and we ask guests to avoid sportswear and beachwear in the evening service.

Can you handle dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us at least 48 hours ahead and the kitchen will rebuild the menu rather than remove courses. We cook full vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free tasting menus nightly.

Are children welcome?

Children are welcome before 7 PM at every branch. After 7 PM we keep the room adults-only so the pacing holds.

Is parking available?

Valet runs from 6 PM at the Astor, Marina, and Colaba rooms. All other branches sit within a short walk of public parking.

02

Reservations

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks for weekends, and roughly six weeks for the chef counter. Weekday tables often open the same day.

Do you take deposits?

A deposit applies to the chef table and all private dining bookings. It comes straight off your final bill.

What is the cancellation window?

Cancel or amend free of charge up to 48 hours before service. Inside that window the deposit is retained.

Can I request a specific table?

Leave a note on your booking and the concierge will do their best. Counter and window seats are allocated on the day.

03

Events & Private Dining

What is the minimum spend?

It varies by room, night, and season. The events team will quote a firm figure with your menu proposal.

Can we bring our own wine?

Yes, up to two bottles per table with a corkage fee, provided the label is not already on our list.

Do you cater outside the restaurant?

For full buyouts and partner venues only. Send the brief to the concierge desk and we will tell you honestly if it fits.

Is the room accessible?

Every branch has step-free access, accessible restrooms, and adjustable seating. Tell us in advance and we will set the room accordingly.

04

Payment, Vouchers & Billing

Is service included in the price?

Yes. Menu prices include service, and nothing is added to the bill afterwards beyond local tax. Tipping is genuinely optional and split across the whole team when it happens.

Can we split the bill?

Across up to six cards without any fuss. Splitting by individual item takes longer, so tell your server before dessert rather than at the end.

How do gift vouchers work?

They are bought against an experience rather than a cash balance, valid twenty-four months, usable at any of the seven rooms, and replaced free if lost.

Do you invoice companies?

Corporate accounts are set up through accounts@stackly.example and settle monthly on a single invoice, including multi-city itineraries.

05

Accessibility & Comfort

Is the restaurant step-free?

Every branch has a step-free entrance, accessible restrooms on the ground floor, and adjustable seating. Tell us in advance and the room is set before you arrive.

Do you have large-print menus?

Large-print and braille menus are carried at every branch as standard. You should not have to ask, but if one is not offered, please do.

Can you seat an assistance dog?

Always, at any table, with water brought without being asked. Let us know when booking so we can allocate the right amount of space.

Is the room very loud?

The flagship runs around 68 decibels at full capacity, which is quieter than most rooms its size. Ask for a corner banquette if you would prefer quieter still.
