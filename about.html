About Us

Stackly blends precise hospitality with an intimate, dark-luxury dining language.

Luxury Restaurant

A dining room built around restraint.

Founded for guests who want food, room, light, and sound to feel composed rather than loud.

Stackly opened in 2009 with twelve seats and a single fire. We still cook the way we did then: a short menu, produce bought the same week, and a brigade that plates every course within arm's reach of the guest. What changed is the number of rooms, not the standard inside them.

What We Stand For

Three commitments we do not trade away.

01

Sourcing before technique

We buy from thirty-one growers and four day boats. If the delivery is wrong, the dish comes off the menu that night rather than getting fixed in the pan.

02

Craft you can watch

Every branch has an open pass. Stocks run for eighteen hours, pastry is laminated in-house, and nothing arrives at the door pre-portioned.

03

Service without theatre

Our floor team is trained to read a table and then leave it alone. Attentive when you look up, invisible when you are mid-sentence.

The Room

Designed to be quiet at full capacity.

A hundred and twenty covers should not sound like it. Most of the build budget went into things guests never consciously notice.

Acoustic baffling sits above the ceiling panels, the banquettes are filled rather than sprung, and every table is weighted so it cannot rock. Lighting runs at 2200K and drops in four stages across the evening, so the room dims with the service rather than at a switch.

The bar was moved away from the dining floor in the 2023 rebuild for exactly one reason: you could hear the ice well from table nine.

Responsibility

Waste

Under 4% food waste

Measured weekly against purchase weight. Trim becomes stock, stock becomes glaze, and what is left goes to compost through a local partner.
