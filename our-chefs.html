How We Cook

Short menu, deep bench.

Nine dishes on the carte means every chef cooks their section hundreds of times a month. Repetition is the point.

The brigade tastes the full menu together every Sunday and argues about seasoning until it is settled. Four of our seven head chefs started here as commis, and we still take two apprentices a year at every branch.

A Day at the Pass

Service is the short part.

Guests see three hours of it. The brigade has been in the building since seven that morning.

Time What Happens
07:00 Deliveries checked and rejected at the door if the quality is wrong
08:30 Stocks skimmed, ferments logged, pastry lamination begins
11:00 Family meal, then the menu briefing for the full floor team
12:00 Lunch service, four courses, through to three o'clock
15:30 Break, then the evening prep and the specials board rewrite
18:00 Dinner service opens, counter seatings at seven and nine
23:15 Kitchen closes, deep clean, and tomorrow's order goes in
