Your Data

Take it with you, or wipe it.

Everything this build knows about you sits in your browser's LocalStorage. There is no server copy to request and nothing to delete on our side.

Export: downloads your profile, favourites, and booking history as a single JSON file.

Import: restores that file on another browser or after clearing your data.

Clear: removes everything immediately. It cannot be undone and there is no backup.
