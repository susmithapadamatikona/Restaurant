Reading the Menu

What the tags actually mean.

Veg / Non-Veg

Dietary marks

Every dish carries its base classification. Vegan and gluten-free versions of most plates exist on request.

Chef pick

The kitchen's choice

Marked by the head chef each week. It is usually whatever arrived best from the market, not the most expensive plate.

Timing

Kitchen minutes

The listed time is how long the dish takes from order to pass, so you can pace a shorter lunch properly.

Price

Inclusive of service

Prices include service. Nothing is added at the end of the bill beyond local tax.

Tasting Menu

Ten courses, roughly three hours.

The full progression is written weekly and runs for the whole table. It is the most complete argument the kitchen can make.

Pairings are offered in two forms: six glasses from the cellar, or a zero-proof flight built from house ferments, teas, and cordials. Both are matched by weight and finish rather than by colour.

Allergens

Ask, and we will rebuild the plate.

Tell us in advance

48 hours for the carte and 72 for the tasting menu gives the kitchen time to plan a parallel course rather than strip one out.

Shared equipment

We cook in one kitchen. We manage cross-contact carefully but cannot promise a fully isolated environment for severe allergies.

Full alternative menus

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, and pescatarian progressions run nightly at every branch.

Drink Alongside

Three ways to pair, none of them compulsory.

The cellar flight

Zero-proof flight
