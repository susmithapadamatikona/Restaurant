How Planning Runs

Four steps, about three weeks.

Step 1

Brief

Guest count, date, occasion, and budget. A fifteen minute call is usually enough.

Step 2

Proposal

Room, draft menu, wine pairings, and a firm cost within two working days.

Step 3

Tasting

For bookings over twenty guests, you and one guest taste the final menu at no charge.

Step 4

Service

We set the room, run the evening, and send a single itemised invoice afterwards.

Private Menus

Three formats, all written from scratch.

Seated

Four to ten courses

The standard format. A fixed progression for the whole table, plated in the kitchen and served together. Best above twelve guests.

Family

Shared centrepieces

Large-format cuts and whole fish carved at the table, with sides passed between guests. Louder, slower, and better for a room that already knows each other.

Standing

Canape and station

Twelve to eighteen bites across the evening with two live stations. For launches and receptions where nobody sits down.

Before You Enquire

The questions the events desk gets most.

Is there a minimum spend?

Yes, and it varies by room, night, and season rather than by a fixed rate card. The proposal quotes one firm figure covering food, drink, and hire together.

When do you need final numbers?

Seventy-two hours before service. You can drop up to 10% of the guest count inside that window without affecting the quote.

Can we run a presentation?

In the Astor Salon, yes. Screen, microphone, and a sound feed you control, tested with your deck on the afternoon of the event.

Do you handle the flowers and printing?

Included as standard. Menus printed with your wording, and floral direction agreed at the proposal stage with our regular florist.

What about a bar tab?

Either a capped tab or full pairings. Both appear as separate lines on the single invoice sent afterwards.

Can we bring a cake or a DJ?

Cakes yes, with a small plating charge. Music depends on the room and the hour, so raise it early and we will tell you honestly.
